VLA Post-Training with Offline Policy Optimization for Bimanual Garment Folding2026.10
  • Task and base model Folded 4 garment categories (long/short sleeves, trousers/shorts) with tianji bimanual dexterous hands; post-trained the Pi0.5 VLA base with multi-view visual and proprioceptive inputs and action-chunk outputs
  • Offline policy optimization (AWR + RECAP) The flow-matching action head outputs only a velocity field with no action density, so log-prob-based on-policy methods do not apply: adopted AWR, reweighting data by exp(advantage) under a KL constraint at the sampling stage, plus RECAP dual-mode training — an unconditional pass learns the original distribution while an advantage-token conditional pass regresses only high-advantage actions and is the sole deployment mode, shifting the distribution toward high-return regions without leaving the action manifold
  • Reward and advantage estimation Built dense intermediate rewards from keypoint checkpoints, reused the success-probability head as a value function and ran multi-step advantage estimation with GAE, turning sparse success into optimizable per-segment advantages
  • Lightweight world model Added multi-task auxiliary heads to the VLA to predict keypoint distances several steps ahead; low-dimensional keypoint-distance sequences replace pixel reconstruction as the dynamics model, giving look-ahead signals without extra inference cost
  • Distributed asynchronous training pipeline Decoupled training and rollout nodes via a model/data repository so they run asynchronously and scale independently; combined domain randomization (texture/lighting/camera/physics), curriculum learning, success replay and hard-example mining to raise data efficiency and robustness
  • Sim2Real and human-in-the-loop Transferred sim-to-real via behavior-cloning fine-tuning and multi-source data mixing, aligning camera poses and execution speed with image augmentation; used DAgger to collect short corrective segments at failures, adding error recovery and mitigating the limited exploration of purely off-policy training
