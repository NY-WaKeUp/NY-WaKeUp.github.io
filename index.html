VLA Post-Training with Offline Policy Optimization for Bimanual Garment Folding2026.10
  • Task and base model Folded 4 garment categories (long/short sleeves, trousers/shorts) with tianji bimanual dexterous hands; post-trained the Pi0.5 VLA base with multi-view visual and proprioceptive inputs and action-chunk outputs
  • Offline policy optimization (AWR + RECAP) The flow-matching head gives only a velocity field with no action density, so log-prob on-policy methods do not apply: AWR reweights data by exp(advantage) under a KL constraint, and RECAP adds dual-mode training — an unconditional pass learns the original distribution, while an advantage-token conditional pass regresses only high-advantage actions and is the sole deployment mode
  • Reward and advantage estimation Built dense intermediate rewards from keypoint checkpoints, reused the success-probability head as a value function and ran multi-step advantage estimation with GAE, turning sparse success into optimizable per-segment advantages
  • Lightweight world model Added multi-task auxiliary heads predicting keypoint distances several steps ahead; keypoint-distance sequences replace pixel reconstruction as the dynamics model, giving look-ahead signals at no extra inference cost
  • Distributed asynchronous training pipeline Decoupled training and rollout nodes via a model/data repository for asynchronous, independently scalable execution; combined domain randomization (texture/lighting/camera/physics), curriculum learning, success replay and hard-example mining
  • Sim2Real and human-in-the-loop Sim-to-real via behavior-cloning fine-tuning and multi-source data mixing, with camera-pose and speed alignment plus image augmentation; DAgger collected short corrective segments at failures, adding error recovery
